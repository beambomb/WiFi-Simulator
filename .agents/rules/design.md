# UI/UX & Flat CAD Design System

## 1. Zero Gradients Rule
- All backgrounds, panels, buttons, badges, and 3D meshes use flat solid colors.
- Heatmap visual: Stepped contour / iso-bands (discrete flat color bands) instead of smooth blurry gradients.

## 2. Solid Color Palette
- Canvas Background: `#1A1D24`
- Major Grid: `#2E3646` (1.0m) | Minor Grid: `#222834` (0.25m)
- Panel Surface: `#20252F` | Panel Border: `#343D4F`
- Text Primary: `#F1F5F9` | Text Secondary: `#94A3B8`
- Accent / Active: `#3B82F6`

## 3. Material Agent Colors
- Reinforced Concrete: `#718096`
- Red Brick: `#C25E40`
- Drywall: `#CBD5E1`
- Solid Wood: `#A2714B`
- Wooden Door: `#855836`
- Clear Glass Window: `#60A5FA` (60% opacity)
- Metal Sheet: `#475569`

## 4. Signal Quality Tiers (Flat Stepped Colors)
- $\ge -50\text{ dBm}$ (Excellent): `#10B981` (Emerald)
- $-51\dots-65\text{ dBm}$ (Good): `#3B82F6` (Blue)
- $-66\dots-75\text{ dBm}$ (Fair): `#F59E0B` (Amber)
- $-76\dots-85\text{ dBm}$ (Poor): `#EF4444` (Red)
- $< -85\text{ dBm}$ (Dead Zone): `#1F2937` (Dark Gray)
